## 1. Question, and what Config B is
- **Question:** does replaying existing training records under family weights taken from a scripted CAGE 4 scenario
  change held-out-family recall at equal false-positive rate?
  - compared with replaying the same number of records without simulator information (B − B0, primary);
  - compared with no replay (B − A, secondary).
- **Role:** Config B is the "adversarial data alone" baseline for RQ1, so that Config C against Config B isolates
  adaptive co-training.
- **What it is:** simulator-conditioned flow replay augmentation. It is reweighting of real development records,
  encoded as integer copies.
  - **CAGE 4 generates no traffic.** No replayed record is described as traffic generated by CAGE.
  - The attacker is scripted, not adaptive, and the blue team sleeps.

## 6. Arms
**The same-records rule:** all three arms have exactly the same distinct training records, Config A's capped
training rows for the fold. Replayed copies are added on top and never displace a real row.

- **A:** Config A, unchanged, fitted through the Config B wrapper (section 7) with zero replay.
- **B, simulator-derived family weights.** For fold seed s, held-out family F and ratio r:
  1. Take the mapped event counts of seed s, per family.
  2. Drop F and any family absent from training. Their events are reported as "excluded: held out".
  3. Set p_f = events_f / Σ events over the remaining mapped families.
  4. Set the requested total R = r × Σ (capped training rows of those families).
  5. Family f requests round(R × p_f) replays. They are dealt cyclically without replacement within f's capped
     training rows, each pass a fresh seeded permutation.
  6. **The cap:** at most 4 replays per training row. The excess is truncated, not redistributed. It is kept as a
     guard even though it does not bind under this mapping.
  7. If no mapped family remains, B adds nothing.
- **B0, uniform over the seen attack-record pool.**
  - **Pool:** every capped attack training row of every seen family, mapped or not.
  - **Total:** exactly B's realized total for the same fold, seed and ratio.
  - **Draws:** cyclic without replacement over the whole pool, each pass a fresh seeded permutation, with the same
    per-row cap.
  - **No simulator information.** If B adds nothing, B0 adds nothing.
- **The pool in nested selection:** the inner fold's capped training rows (`selection.pair_rows`).
- **The pool in shortcut treatments:** replay is computed after the treatment, from the treatment's training rows.
- **Seeds:** replay draws are seeded from the fold seed and recorded.
- **Reported per fold, seed, ratio and arm:**
  - requested, realized and truncated replays per family;
  - excluded and unmapped events (O4 and O6 separately);
  - distinct rows, replays, nominal rows and Kish ESS, per family and for the attack class.

### 6.1 What B − B0 measures in each fold (owner-required limitation)
B0's composition is proportional to family size. Under cleaned counts with the cap applied, the training rows are
about 442 for `bot`, 4,159 for `brute_force`, 100,000 for `dos` (capped) and 4,617 for `portscan`:

| Held out | B's replay composition | B0's expected composition | What B − B0 contrasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bot` | `brute_force` 37%, `portscan` 63% | `dos` 92%, `portscan` 4%, `brute_force` 4% | Mostly replay of the two mapped families against replay that is mostly `dos` |
| `dos` | `brute_force` 37%, `portscan` 63% | `portscan` 50%, `brute_force` 45%, `bot` 5% | The closest test of the simulator split itself. The compositions differ only modestly |
| `brute_force` | `portscan` 100% | `dos` 95%, `portscan` 4%, `bot` <1% | Concentrating replay on the one remaining mapped family |
| `portscan` | `brute_force` 100% | `dos` 96%, `brute_force` 4%, `bot` <1% | The same |

These figures are illustrative, from Config A's cleaned counts. The run reports the realized compositions.

**Fold groups (owner, 2026-11-10).** These three groups are kept separate in every table and statement:

| Group | Outer folds | Inner selection folds (of 12) | What B − B0 is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Two-family** (training holds both mapped families) | `bot`, `dos`, the only such outer folds | 2: (`bot`, `dos`) and (`dos`, `bot`) | The only folds where B's simulator-derived split is active |
| **Single-family** (one mapped family in training) | `brute_force`, `portscan` | 8 | All of B's replay goes to the remaining mapped family. **These folds cannot establish a benefit from simulator-derived weighting.** |
| **Neither-family** (no mapped family in training) | none | 2: (`brute_force`, `portscan`) and (`portscan`, `brute_force`) | **Controls:** B = B0 = A by construction, at every ratio |

An inner fold is written (outer held-out family, inner held-out family). It trains on the other two families.

- **In the single-family folds,** the simulator-derived weights add no distinction beyond the mapping's choice of
  the remaining family. B − B0 there measures family selection, not weighting.
- **Neither-family controls:**
  - B, B0 and A have identical multiplicities there (invariant 4), and so identical training data, so their
    inner recall must be identical.
  - A difference is treated as an invariant failure and stops the run.
  - The selection rule counts these folds as ties, so no candidate wins them.
- **What B − B0 is a contrast between: replay-composition policies.**
  - B − B0 compares two specified policies:
    - "split replay between the mapped seen families by CAGE-derived weights";
    - "spread replay over the seen attack pool in proportion to family size".
  - In the `bot` fold, B0 is about 92% `dos` replay, so the contrast is mostly between replaying the two mapped
    families and replaying mostly `dos`.
  - Results are described as **the effect of these specified replay-composition policies**. They are not an
    isolated, pure causal effect of simulator information or "simulator intelligence".
- **The weights barely vary across fold seeds** (`brute_force` 0.371–0.378). In effect B uses one fixed 37:63
  composition. Even in the two-family folds, B − B0 tests that composition against size-proportional replay. It
  cannot show that information specific to the simulator mattered beyond that composition.

## 7. Fit wrapper and the same-records invariant
- **Config A is not modified.** Config B gets its own wrapper, inside Config B's identity.
- **Preprocessing:**
  - the preprocessor is fitted once, on the fold's distinct training rows, by Config A's own procedure
    (`build_preprocessor(...).fit`);
  - the identical fitted parameters are applied to A, B and B0.
- **Replay encoding: integer copies.** Copies of already preprocessed distinct rows are appended.
  - The forest uses Config A's exact settings, including `class_weight="balanced_subsample"`.
  - No `sample_weight` is used.
  - Each contrast therefore measures family composition within the attack class.
- **Accepted limitation:** with `min_samples_leaf=2`, a record and its copy can form a leaf on their own. This is
  reported with the results.

**Invariant checks.** They are automated, pass or fail, and run before every fit. **Any failure stops execution**,
with no correction, retry or fallback:
1. **Distinct records:** A, B and B0 have the same set of distinct training row indices, and every replayed index
   lies in A's capped rows for that fold and treatment.
2. **Excluded rows:** no replayed index is a validation or test row, a held-out-family row, or a row the cap left
   out. No exact feature vector of a replayed row appears in the fold's test rows.
3. **Preprocessing:** the fitted parameters are bit-identical across arms, and so is the transformed matrix of
   distinct rows.
4. **Multiplicities:** every multiplicity is at least 1. Σ(multiplicity − 1) equals B's realized total, for B and
   for B0. A's multiplicities are all 1.
5. **Settings:** the forest settings, seed and fixed-order scoring equal Config A's frozen ones.
6. **Preprocessing gate:** before the first fit of every stage, the wrapper's preprocessor and its transformed
   distinct rows must equal, bit for bit, what Config A's `RandomForestBaseline.fit` path produces on the same rows.

**Mandatory tests**, on synthetic data in the Aegis suite. All must pass before any stage runs.
`tests/test_safeguards.py`'s frozen-identity tests must still pass.

| Test | What it proves |
|---|---|
| T1 | Preprocessing reproduction: bit-identical to Config A's path, including the `min_frequency=20` boundary and missing values |
| T2 | A-arm equivalence: zero replay gives scores bit-identical to Config A's `RandomForestBaseline` for any `n_jobs` |
| T3 | Copies, not weights: B's matrix is A's plus exact copies; no `sample_weight`; Config A's forest parameters |
| T4 | Copies never refit preprocessing, even when they push a rare category past 20 |
| T5 | Pool boundaries in outer folds, inner folds and every shortcut treatment |
| T6 | Allocation follows the weights; the cap truncates without redistributing; B0 matches B's realized total from the whole seen pool; both are seeded and reproducible |
| T7 | Each invariant fed a crafted violation raises and stops, leaving no partial outputs |
| T8 | Outcome counting (O1–O6) on a synthetic action log |
| T9 | Seed derivation: unique for fold seeds 0–4 and episodes 0–49, and rejects an episode index ≥ 1000 |
| T10 | The Kish ESS matches the closed form for cyclic replays and never exceeds the distinct row count |

## 8. Ratio selection (nested, equal rigor for B and B0)
- **Candidates:** 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0. **The default is 0.25.**
- **B and B0 each select their own ratio,** by Config A's inner leave-one-family-out rule on training and
  validation rows only, at 1% target FPR:
  - a challenger replaces the default only if its mean inner recall is higher **and** it wins on a strict majority
    of inner families;
  - a tie on a family is not a win;
  - among challengers that pass, the highest mean wins, and ties go to the one listed first.
- **Matched totals:** in each inner fold, B0 at r adds exactly B's realized total at r.
- **In the outer fold,** B0's total is B's realized total at B0's own selected ratio.
- **If the arms select different ratios,** that is reported with each arm's added total. B − B0 is then read as
  confounding amount with composition.
- B0 is never given B's choice. Outer families are only reported, never used in selection.

## 9. Analysis rules
### 9.1 Primary
- **Outcome:** raw-view recall on F's test rows, at the threshold from validation benign at **1% target FPR**, for
  each held-out family F and seed s ∈ 0–4.
- **Primary contrast:** d_s = recall_B − recall_B0, paired by seed, **reported separately for each held-out family.**
- **Statistics:**
  - mean d over the 5 seeds;
  - 95% bootstrap interval (10,000 resamples over seeds);
  - two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test;
  - Cohen's d_z.
- **Five seeds, stated with every result.**
  - **The smallest attainable two-sided Wilcoxon p with 5 seeds is 0.0625.** No test can reach 0.05.
  - A bootstrap interval over 5 seeds is coarse, and is described as such.
  - **What is read first:** the effect size (mean d and Cohen's d_z) and its interval. The p-value is reported
    but never makes a claim on its own.
- **Classification of each family's contrast (owner, 2026-11-10):**
  - *positive* if the 95% interval lies entirely above zero;
  - *negative* if it lies entirely below zero;
  - *inconclusive* if it includes zero.
  - **No numerical minimum effect size is set.** The shortcut rule's 0.10 is not a Config B effect-size threshold.
  - **Always reported:** the actual mean difference, its interval and d_z, whatever the classification.
  - **Distinguishable is not important.** A *positive* or *negative* classification means only that the interval
    excludes zero. Practical importance is discussed separately, from the size of the effect, and never inferred
    from the classification.
- **Fold groups (6.1), never pooled:**
  - **Two-family folds** (`bot`, `dos`): the only outer folds where B's simulator-derived split is active.
  - **Single-family folds** (`brute_force`, `portscan`): B − B0 measures concentration on the remaining mapped
    family. **They cannot establish a benefit from simulator-derived weighting, and none is claimed from them.**
  - **Neither-family folds** (inner selection only): controls where B = B0 = A, reported with the selection
    results.
  - **No pooled estimate** across held-out families is computed or reported as a weighting effect.
- **What would support "the CAGE-derived composition policy outperformed size-proportional replay on family F"**
  (and nothing weaker supports it): a *positive* B − B0 in a two-family fold.
  - It is stated as the effect of the specified replay-composition policies, with the limits in 6.1. It is not
    stated as a pure causal effect of simulator information.
- **An *inconclusive* B − B0** is reported as no detectable difference between the two policies on that family. It
  is not reported as evidence that they are equal.

### 9.2 Secondary
- **B − A and B0 − A,** per family, with the same statistics and classification.
- **The difference in degradation** (seen recall minus held-out recall), per contrast.
  - The seen model is the fold-seed fit with every family seen. B's seen fit replays both mapped families.
- **The same contrasts at 0.1% target FPR,** and in the overlap-excluded and deduplicated views.
- **Realized FPR, PR-AUC, and precision** at 1% and 0.1% prevalence.
- **Selected ratios** per arm, fold and seed, with the inner results split by fold group (6.1).
- **Replay accounting and ESS** (section 6).

### 9.3 Shortcut audit
- **Arms:** applied to A (fresh, under `25685b84dd83f65c`), to B and to B0.
- **Treatments:** the primary treatments, `remove_matching_signatures`, `no_tcp_window`,
  `neutralize_missing_markers` and `all_controls`. `missingness_only` is reported outside the rule.
- **Rule, unchanged:** a family is shortcut-dependent if the mean per-seed drop in raw recall at 1% target FPR is at
  least 0.10 and its 95% bootstrap interval lies above zero.
  - A family whose reference recall is under 0.10 is *not testable*.
  - Every comparison is counted, with no correction.
- **Reporting:** verdicts are reported per arm, and differences between arms descriptively.
- **History:** the Phase 1 verdicts (CSE pre-registration 6.3) are not revised. The fresh A audit is reported beside
  them, and any difference is reported, not corrected.

### 9.4 Reporting
- **Everything is reported**, including null, negative and failed results.
- **With every result:**
  - the limitations in 6.1;
  - the thin intervention: mapped actions are 32,215 of 297,626 red actions (10.8%), or 11.4% counting the rejected
    `SQLInjection`. `Impact` and `DegradeServices` make up 61%;
  - the single-dataset scope (section 2);
  - a description of the scenario as **CAGE 4 scripted red behaviour against a sleeping blue, not a representative
    real-world attacker population.**

### 9.5 Claims
- **Supported, if the data show it:** the sentence in design 2.6, restricted to improved CICIDS2017, read per
  held-out family with 6.1.
- **Not supported:**
  - an isolated causal effect of simulator information ("simulator intelligence"), as opposed to the effect of the
    specified composition policies;
  - practical importance inferred from a *positive* or *negative* classification alone;
  - that CAGE produced traffic;
  - that simulated attacks resemble real ones;
  - that the scenario mix reflects real attackers;
  - that individual records are more realistic;
  - robustness to adaptive attackers;
  - cross-dataset validation;
  - a general weighting benefit from the single-family folds.

## 10. Determinism
- **The rerun (stage 5):** a same-environment rerun of the A, B and B0 outer folds for every seed, through the
  Config B code path, with the selected ratios.
- **Criterion:** every recorded column except timing columns must match exactly: recall, realized FPR, AUC, PR-AUC,
  precision, row hashes, replay counts and multiplicities. That is the expectation under fixed-order scoring and a
  fixed environment.
  - No tolerance is set in advance.
  - A mismatch is reported as a failed determinism check, with every differing column, its row and both values.
    Results are then labelled not reproduced.
  - No tolerance is added after the fact. What follows a failure is the owner's decision.
- **Results are not reported as final** before stage 5 has run.
